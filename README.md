# Mountains-runner

**Camp With a Friend** is a low-poly, open-world mountain driving game that runs in the browser. Open `index.html` to play. There is nothing to install.

## What's in it
- **Big map**: 2.4 km across, ringed by snowy mountains. Only the ground and trees near you are drawn, so it stays smooth on phones.
- **Windy valley highway**: a 6.7 km paved loop with about 70 bends, S-curves and hairpins (tightest 16 m radius), lane markings, reflector posts, and six other vehicles driving on it.
- **Ridge trail**: a rocky dirt trail with switchbacks up to Lookout Peak. Use 4L low range to crawl over the rocks.
- **Big air**: floaty air physics give big hang time over mountain crests and bumps, with an airtime and height callout when you land.
- **Your own camp**: name it on the start screen, and the name appears on a wooden sign at the entrance. It has tents, a campfire, and a picnic table. Park here to repair damage.
- **A friend**: name them on the start screen. They sit by the fire and wave when you arrive. Press E near them to invite them along. They follow you on foot, ride along in the passenger seat, and react to your driving.
- **Five vehicles**: Ridgeback 4x4 (overland SUV), Coupe '87 (black E30-style coupe with the M20 2.5 straight-six sound from the M20 Engine Sound page, 5-speed manual), GT Turbo 98 (Supra-style big-turbo coupe), Pocket 50 Turbo (P50-style microcar with a turbo bigger than the car: about 10,000 km/h, blasts through obstacles and wraps around the map edge), and Haulmark Pickup (4x4 truck).
- **Real-sounding engines**: each engine is synthesized from its cylinder count and rpm: the E30 M3-style four, turbo six, V8, diesels and a two-stroke. Turbo cars whistle and make a blow-off sound when you lift off.
- **Your own E30 sound**: on the start screen, pick a sound or video file from your device. The game finds its steadiest engine note, loops it and re-pitches it to the E30's rpm. The file stays on your device and is remembered in your browser.
- **Manual gearbox**: the E30 is manual by default, and any car can switch with M. It has a tachometer and a rev limiter.
- **On foot**: get out of any vehicle to walk, run, and jump, then get into another vehicle.
- **Day/night cycle**: working headlights, stars, and a lit campfire at night.
- **Dashboard**: roll and pitch gauges, wheel contact, altitude, compass, minimap, speed, gear, 2H/4H/4L, damage, and grade.

## Controls
| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| W / S | Gas · brake / reverse (walk on foot) |
| A / D | Steer |
| E | Get in / get out · talk to your friend |
| Space | Handbrake · jump on foot |
| Shift | Run |
| F | Cycle 2H / 4H / 4L |
| M | Manual / automatic gearbox (or tap the gear) |
| X / Z | Shift up / down (manual) |
| L | Headlights |
| C | Camera (chase / far / driver's view / top-down) |
| R | Recover a stuck or rolled vehicle |
| T | Skip one hour |
| Esc | Pause |

Drag to look around and scroll to zoom.

## Playing with a controller
Any standard game controller works (Xbox, PlayStation, Switch Pro and others, over Bluetooth or USB). Press a button and the on-screen touch controls step aside.

| Control | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Left stick | Steer · walk |
| Right stick | Look around |
| RT / LT | Gas / brake |
| A | Get in or out · talk to your friend · set up or use your tent |
| B | Handbrake · jump on foot |
| X | Clutch (hold) |
| Y | Camera |
| RB / LB | Shift up / down |
| D-pad | Up 4x4 mode · Down recover · Left manual/auto · Right lights |
| Start | Pause / resume |

On the start screen, the d-pad picks a car and A starts. In the tent menu, A sleeps, Y packs up, B closes. Crashes and hard landings rumble the controller.

## Playing on a phone
Camp With a Friend is built for phones held sideways:
- **Side menu**: Menu, Camera, Spawn (puts your car on the nearest road with you in it) and Map (full-screen map with home, camp, the peak, your tent and traffic).
- **Speedometer**: a compact speed pill at the bottom with gear, AUTO/MANUAL, a rev bar, and TCS/ABS lights that come on when the wheels slip or you brake hard.
- **Controls**: steer with the ◀ ▶ buttons (on foot they become a ◀ ▲ ▼ ▶ pad), GAS and BRAKE pedals on the right. USE gets in and out of vehicles and talks to your friend. HAND is the handbrake, and it becomes JUMP on foot. UP and DN shift gears in manual. RESET recovers a stuck car.
- **Camera**: drag the screen to look around and pinch to zoom.
- **Screen**: Start goes full screen and locks landscape where the phone allows it, and the screen stays awake while you play.
- **Rotate prompt**: if you hold the phone upright, a prompt asks you to turn it sideways. You can still choose to play upright.
- **Graphics**: on slower phones, the game automatically lowers resolution and turns off shadows to stay smooth.
- **Vibration**: crashes, hard landings, gear shifts and instant stops vibrate on phones that support it.
